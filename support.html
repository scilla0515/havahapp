Common questions

How do I correct my period start date?

Go to the Log tab, press and hold the correct day on the calendar, and choose “Period Started.” Your other logged cycles stay as they are.

How do I record when my period ended?

On the Log tab, press and hold the last day of your period and choose “Period Ended.” Havah uses the end dates you confirm to learn your typical period length.

Where is my information stored?

On your device. Havah does not send your cycle information to a Havah server, so we cannot restore it if you delete it or lose your device.

How do I delete my data?

Use the reset option in Havah to remove locally stored information, or delete the app from your device.

Are Havah’s predictions medical advice?

No. Predictions are estimates based on what you log. They are not medical advice, a diagnostic tool, or a method of contraception. Please talk to your healthcare provider about medical decisions.
