Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

Havah ("Havah," "we," "us," or "our") is a cycle and wellness application designed to help women understand, track, and reflect on their menstrual cycles and wellness patterns.

We believe information about a woman's body and health should be handled with care, respect, and transparency. Havah is designed with a local-first privacy model. In the current version of Havah, the cycle and wellness information you enter into the application is processed and stored on your device rather than transmitted to a Havah server.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Havah handles, how that information is used, how it is stored, and the choices available to you.

1. Information You Provide to Havah

Depending on the features you use, Havah may allow you to enter and store the following information.

Cycle and menstrual information

This may include:

  • Period start dates

  • Period end dates

  • Cycle length

  • Period length

  • Menstrual flow information

  • Cycle phase information

  • Historical cycle information

  • Fertile-window estimates

  • Ovulation estimates

  • Corrections to previously predicted or recorded period dates

Wellness information

This may include:

  • Symptoms

  • Mood

  • Basal body temperature (BBT)

  • Intimacy information

  • Personal notes and reflections

  • Wellness intentions

  • Other information you voluntarily enter into Havah

Personal preferences and settings

This may include:

  • Your name

  • Cycle preferences

  • Period-length preferences

  • Cycle-length preferences

  • Conception-related preferences

  • Notification preferences

  • Other settings you choose within Havah

You decide what information to enter into Havah.

2. How Havah Uses Your Information

Havah uses information you provide to operate and personalize the application.

This includes using information on your device to:

  • Calculate your current cycle day

  • Estimate upcoming cycle phases

  • Display menstrual, follicular, ovulatory, and luteal phases

  • Estimate upcoming periods

  • Recalculate predictions when you correct a period date

  • Learn from confirmed historical cycle information

  • Provide cycle and wellness insights

  • Display your cycle history

  • Support cycle and wellness logging

  • Support journaling and reflection features

  • Provide reminders you choose to receive

Havah's cycle-learning system is designed to distinguish between information you have actually confirmed and predictions generated by the application.

Predicted information is not treated as confirmed historical information.

3. How Havah Stores Your Information

Havah is designed as a local-first application.

In the current version of Havah, cycle and wellness information is stored locally on your device using the application's local storage systems.

Havah currently uses local database storage for cycle information and secure device storage for certain personal settings.

Havah does not currently operate a cloud database or Havah server that stores your cycle history.

Based on the current version of the application, Havah does not transmit your cycle or wellness history to a Havah server.

Because your information is stored locally, Havah generally does not have access to a cloud copy of your cycle history.

4. Notifications

Havah includes notification functionality provided by your device's operating system. In the current version of Havah, this functionality is configured but not actively used to schedule cycle, period, phase, or logging reminders.

If Havah introduces scheduled notifications in a future update — such as reminders relating to your cycle, period, cycle phases, or logging — this Privacy Policy will be updated before that feature becomes available to describe what information those notifications may reference and how you can control or disable them.

You can manage notification permissions for Havah at any time through your iPhone's notification settings.

5. Sharing and Sale of Information

Havah does not sell your personal information.

Havah does not currently sell, rent, or provide your cycle or wellness information to advertisers or data brokers.

Havah does not currently use third-party advertising or analytics services to create advertising profiles based on your cycle or wellness information.

Havah's current architecture does not include a Havah cloud database for storing your cycle or wellness history.

Apple may independently process information in connection with operating iOS, the App Store, notifications, payments, and other Apple services. Apple's processing of information is governed by Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

If Havah introduces a third-party service that receives personal or health-related information from Havah, we will update this Privacy Policy and applicable privacy disclosures to explain that practice.

6. Health and Wellness Information

Havah allows you to record information relating to your menstrual cycle and wellness.

We understand that this information can be sensitive. Havah does not use your cycle or wellness information for advertising or sale to third parties.

Havah's current design is intended to keep this information on your device.

Apple's platform services may independently process information as necessary to provide their respective services.

7. Period and Cycle Predictions

Havah provides estimates based on information you provide and the application's cycle calculations.

These may include:

  • Expected period dates

  • Cycle phase estimates

  • Ovulation estimates

  • Fertile-window estimates

  • Cycle-pattern insights

Predictions may differ from your actual biological experience.

As you record additional confirmed cycle history, Havah may use that information to refine future estimates.

However, Havah's predictions are estimates and are not guaranteed to be accurate.

8. Medical and Wellness Disclaimer

Havah is a cycle tracking and wellness application, not a medical device or medical provider.

Information provided by Havah is for general informational and wellness purposes and should not be considered medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for professional medical care.

Havah does not guarantee the accuracy of:

  • Period predictions

  • Ovulation predictions

  • Fertile-window estimates

  • Cycle-length estimates

  • Wellness recommendations

  • Other information generated by the application

You should not rely solely on Havah for decisions involving:

  • Pregnancy

  • Contraception

  • Fertility

  • Medical diagnosis

  • Treatment

  • Medication

  • Any other medical decision

If you have questions or concerns about your health, menstrual cycle, fertility, pregnancy, symptoms, or other medical matters, consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

Apple's App Review Guidelines apply additional scrutiny to health applications that provide potentially inaccurate medical information, so Havah's product language should remain consistent with this distinction between wellness estimates and medical advice.

9. Your Control Over Your Information

Havah gives you control over information you enter into the application.

Depending on the feature, you can:

  • Correct your period start date

  • Correct your period end date

  • Edit logged information

  • Change notification preferences

  • View information stored by Havah

  • Reset your Havah data

Havah's reset functionality is designed to remove locally stored Havah information from your device.

Because Havah currently does not maintain a cloud copy of your cycle history, Havah cannot restore information that you permanently delete from your device.

10. Data Retention

Because Havah currently stores your cycle and wellness information locally on your device, that information generally remains on your device until you delete it, reset the application data, or remove the application and its associated data.

Havah does not currently maintain a separate cloud copy of your cycle history.

If you permanently delete locally stored Havah information, that information may not be recoverable.

11. Data Security

Havah uses available device-level storage mechanisms, including secure device storage, to protect certain information stored locally.

However, no electronic storage system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your Apple device, including using an appropriate device passcode, Face ID, Touch ID where available, and current operating-system updates.

12. Children's Privacy

Havah is not directed toward children.

We do not knowingly design Havah to collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Havah inappropriately, please contact us using the information below.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Havah's features, technology, legal obligations, or data practices change.

When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within Havah or through another appropriate channel.

You should periodically review this Privacy Policy to remain informed about Havah's privacy practices.
